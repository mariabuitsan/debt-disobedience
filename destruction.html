Why Colombia? Why Now?

The right-wing government in power wants to sell the land that belongs to rural and indigenous communities to foreign investement for enriching themselves with monocrop production, building big data centers and mega tourism complexes. The real "miracle" of these projects is how fast they will fill the pockets of the corporate firms like JP Morgan and Citi Group while dispossessing the Colombian people and furthering the climate crisis
